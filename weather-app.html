About Weatherline

Forecasts for wherever you are.

Weatherline is a simple way to compare the weather in the places that matter to you. Switch between current conditions, hourly details, and a seven-day outlook; save cities to follow them side by side, or explore their locations and weather markers on the map. Select a map marker to see its conditions and open that city’s forecast. City search and forecast data are provided by Open-Meteo. Times use each location’s local time, temperatures are shown in Celsius, and wind speed is shown in kilometers per hour. Saved cities stay in this browser.
